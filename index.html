Privacy Policy

Clerkly AI · Last updated: September 27, 2026

This policy explains how Clerkly AI handles information when a business owner or staff member uses the service through WhatsApp. Clerkly AI is an early-stage service operated under the name “Clerkly AI”; that name does not represent a registered legal entity.

Information handled

When you message Clerkly AI, WhatsApp and Meta provide the message and related delivery information, such as the sender's WhatsApp number or account identifier, message time, and message contents. If you record a purchase or sale, the service keeps the item name, quantity, amount, transaction type, and time so it can update stock and calculate reports.

The current prototype stores product and transaction records in a local SQLite ledger on the computer or server used to run it. The business operating that installation is responsible for the device, backups, access controls, and deletion of its ledger. The current prototype does not save incoming image files or extract information from images. Do not send sensitive personal, payment-card, identity, or health information.

How information is used

Information is used to reply to WhatsApp messages, record stock purchases and sales, maintain inventory counts, and prepare bookkeeping summaries such as estimated gross profit. It is not used for advertising or sold to data brokers.

Service providers

WhatsApp messages are transmitted through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. Meta processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. The current prototype does not send ledger records to a separate AI model provider. If image reading or another AI provider is added, this policy must be updated before that feature is enabled to identify what is sent and why.

Retention and deletion

Ledger records remain in the business operator's database until that operator deletes them. WhatsApp and Meta may retain information under their own policies. To ask about access, correction, or deletion of information handled by Clerkly AI, contact the email below and include the WhatsApp number you used. The operator may need to verify that the request is yours.

Security and children

The service is intended for business bookkeeping and is not designed for children. No internet service can promise perfect security. The business operator should limit access to its account and device and should not use the prototype as its only accounting record.
